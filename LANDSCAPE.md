> **Methodology note.** The registry's aggregate-analysis tool caps at 5,000 studies per analysis. The full 11,977-record set was therefore aggregated by running identical analyses over disjoint partitions (by overall status; within the large *Completed* bucket by study type and sponsor class) and summing the registry-returned bucket counts. Every figure below is a registry-returned bucket count; partitions are mutually exclusive and sum exactly to the totals shown. No rounding or estimation has been applied.

## 2. Priority classes — largest recruiting, industry-sponsored trials

Selection criteria applied within each class:

- **Condition:** type 2 diabetes mellitus, with a GLP-1 receptor agonist (respectively SGLT2 inhibitor) agent named in the registry intervention field — combination regimens and comparators included
- **Overall status:** Recruiting
- **Lead sponsor class:** Industry
- **Ranking:** by registry enrollment figure; the three largest trials are retained

Class membership was established from the intervention records themselves (drug names plus class labels), because most registrants name the specific agent rather than the class.

#### GLP-1 receptor agonists — top 3

1. **NCT05663515 — EXCEED (AstraZeneca).** Pan-European post-authorisation safety study of pancreatic-cancer risk in T2DM patients initiating **exenatide** (Byetta / Bydureon) versus non–GLP-1 RA glucose-lowering drugs. Non-interventional, retrospective-cohort database study across 7 European countries; 24,000 estimated enrollment; started 2024-09-30; estimated completion 2026-10-01.
2. **NCT07247084 — SURMOUNT-REAL UK (Eli Lilly and Company).** **Tirzepatide** (dual GIP/GLP-1 RA) versus standard of care in adults with obesity and without diabetes; Phase 4; 3,000 participants; started 2025-11-24.
3. **NCT07481747 — SURMOUNT-1 (Hudson Biotech).** Once-weekly **tirzepatide** 5/10/15 mg versus placebo, each with reduced-calorie diet and increased activity, in adults with obesity or overweight with weight-related comorbidities; randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 3; 2,539 participants; started 2026-02-02.

*Borderline exclusion: NCT07564414 (Novo Nordisk, CagriSema vs semaglutide, 2,500, Phase 3) is the next-largest trial in this class but lists obesity ± type 2 diabetes as its conditions — type 2 diabetes mellitus is not in the registry condition field, so it falls outside the condition-scoped set.*

#### SGLT2 inhibitors — top 3

1. **NCT06647888 — (Daewoong Pharmaceutical Co. LTD.).** Multi-center observational study of **enavogliflozin** (Envlo / Envlomet SR, incl. enavogliflozin/metformin) in T2DM with hypertension or dyslipidemia; 12,000 estimated enrollment; started 2024-12-03; estimated completion 2026-06-30.
2. **NCT07064473 — EASi-PROTKT (Boehringer Ingelheim).** Phase 3 double-blind superiority trial of oral **vicadrostat** (BI 690517) added to **empagliflozin** versus placebo plus empagliflozin in T2DM, hypertension and established cardiovascular disease (no heart failure); 11,800 participants; started 2025-07-22.
3. **NCT06838286 — TRIO (Chong Kun Dang Pharmaceutical).** Multi-center, prospective, non-interventional observational study of OAD triple therapy (metformin-based combinations adding an **SGLT2 inhibitor**, DPP-4 inhibitor or TZD) in Korean T2DM patients; 10,000 estimated enrollment; started 2024-01-02; estimated completion 2029-12-31.

*Borderline exclusion: NCT06649162 (Boryung Pharmaceutical, dapagliflozin/pioglitazone, 10,000) is the next-largest trial in this class.*

## 3. Worked example — SUNSHINE (NCT01608724), verified from the registry record

Full record pulled from ClinicalTrials.gov on 2026-10-22 ([registry record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01608724)).

| Field | Registry value |
|---|---|
| NCT ID | NCT01608724 |
| Acronym / org study ID | SUNSHINE / D1680L00008 |
| Brief title | The Efficacy and Safety Study in Patients With Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus |
| Official title | A Multicenter, Single Arm, Cohort Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Saxagliptin 5mg, Once Daily for 24 Weeks, in Patients With Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus |
| Lead sponsor | AstraZeneca (industry); responsible party: sponsor |
| Overall official | Simon Fisher, AstraZeneca (study director) |
| Current overall status | **Completed** (status verified 2015-12) |
| Study type | Interventional |
| Phase | Phase 4 |
| Design | Non-randomized; single-group assignment; treatment purpose; open label (masking: none) |
| Enrollment | 2,165 participants (actual) |
| Intervention | Saxagliptin (Onglyza), oral 5 mg once daily (Q.D.) for 24 weeks, single open-label experimental arm |
| Condition | Diabetes Mellitus, Non-Insulin-Dependent (keyword: Type 2 DM) |
| Start date | 2012-11 |
| Primary completion | 2014-05 (actual) |
| Study completion | 2014-05 (actual) |
| First posted | 2012-05-31 (estimated) |
| Results first posted | 2016-02-02 (estimated); last update posted 2016-02-02 |
| Data monitoring committee | None |
| Expanded access | No |

**Primary outcome:** absolute change from baseline in HbA1c (weeks 6, 12 and 24).
**Secondary outcomes:** proportion of patients achieving HbA1c <7% (weeks 6, 12, 24); change from baseline in fasting plasma glucose (weeks 6, 12, 18, 24); change from baseline in 2-hour postprandial plasma glucose (week 24).

**Key eligibility:** adults ≥18 years (18–99), HbA1c >7.5% and ≤11.0%, treatment-naive or on stable metformin monotherapy for ≥8 weeks. Key exclusions: pregnancy/breastfeeding; insulin therapy within one year of enrollment; previous treatment with any DPP-4 inhibitor or GLP-1 analogue; other antihyperglycemic therapy within 8 weeks (12 weeks for prior TZDs); systemic glucocorticoids (other than replacement).

**Locations:** multicenter study conducted in China (sites include Baotou, Beijing, Benxi, Changchun, Chengdu, Chongqing, Dalian, Dongguan, among others).

**Citation note for the deck:** SUNSHINE is an open-label, single-arm Phase 4 cohort study (n = 2,165 actual) of saxagliptin 5 mg once daily for 24 weeks, completed May 2014. It is **not** a randomized controlled trial — frame it accordingly when used as the worked example.

## 4. Caveats

- All figures are as returned by ClinicalTrials.gov on **2026-10-22**; the registry is a live database and counts will drift between refreshes.
- The analysis tool's 5,000-study cap required partitioned aggregation (see methodology note); bucket counts were summed across disjoint partitions and reconcile exactly to the registry totals shown.
- Phase counts exceed study counts because multi-phase records (678) are counted in each phase they carry; the phase table reports assignments, not unique studies.
- Country counts are site-level: a multinational trial is counted once per country hosting sites.
- Class watchlists are scoped to the condition search plus a class agent in the intervention field; trials naming only a comparator or a non-T2DM indication are excluded by design (see borderline notes in section 2).
