### Selection notes

- **NCT01608724 (SUNSHINE)** was fixed by request (AstraZeneca saxagliptin study; registry acronym "SUNSHINE"; Phase 4; enrollment 2,165; completed May 2014).
- The other four were selected from **currently recruiting, industry-sponsored** type 2 diabetes studies on ClinicalTrials.gov, chosen to span the major competitive archetypes: oral small-molecule CV outcome trial (Boehringer Ingelheim vicadrostat + empagliflozin), oral GLP-1 small molecule (AstraZeneca elecoglipron), incretin obesity/T2D combination (Novo Nordisk CagriSema vs semaglutide), and real-world evidence on an approved non-steroidal MRA in diabetic kidney disease (Bayer finerenone).
- Each profiled trial has a matching sub-issue under the **Trial profiles** parent issue, titled with its NCT number.
