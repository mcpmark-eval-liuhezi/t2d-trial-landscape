## 3. SUNSHINE citation check — NCT01608724

- **Official title:** A Multicenter, Single Arm, Cohort Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Saxagliptin 5mg, Once Daily for 24 Weeks, in Patients With Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus
- **Sponsor:** AstraZeneca (Industry)
- **Enrollment:** 2,165 (actual)
- **Current status:** COMPLETED

Citation check: sponsor (AstraZeneca) and enrollment (2,165, actual) are consistent with the registry record. The trial is no longer active — status is COMPLETED (primary completion 2014-05), so the citation does not need correcting for sponsor or enrollment, but it should be described as a completed study rather than an ongoing one.
