---

## Methodology

The registry's analysis tool caps a single analysis at 5,000 studies, so the full T2D population could not be analyzed in one pass. Every cut in this brief was instead taken directly from the registry's own counts for the exact condition query ("Type 2 Diabetes"), one count per filter, each retrieved live on 2026-10-24 — so each figure below is the registry's own number as returned that day. Every cut was cross-checked against the unscoped condition-query total of 12,017.

## 4. Three largest recruiting, industry-sponsored T2D trials

There are **181** currently recruiting, industry-sponsored T2D trials in the registry. The three largest by enrollment:

### 1. NCT06278207 — FINEROD
- **NCT ID:** NCT06278207
- **Official title:** Finerenone Research of Outcomes and Drug Utilization
- **Sponsor:** Bayer (Industry)
- **Enrollment:** 50,000 (estimated)
- **Status:** Recruiting

Observational, retrospective cohort study of finerenone (Kerendia) use in chronic kidney disease with type 2 diabetes under real-world conditions, using electronic health record and claims data from the US and Asia.

### 2. NCT03549754 — iCaReMe
- **NCT ID:** NCT03549754
- **Official title:** Real-world Multinational Registry to Determine Management and Quality of Care of Patients With Type 2 Diabetes, Hypertension, Heart Failure and/or Chronic Kidney Diseases
- **Sponsor:** AstraZeneca (Industry)
- **Enrollment:** 35,000 (estimated)
- **Status:** Recruiting

Multinational real-world evidence registry across type 2 diabetes, hypertension, heart failure, and chronic kidney disease cohorts.

### 3. NCT05663515 — EXCEED
- **NCT ID:** NCT05663515
- **Official title:** EXCEED - A Pan-European Post-Authorisation Safety Study: Risk of Pancreatic Cancer Among Type 2 Diabetes Patients Who Initiated Exenatide as Compared With Those Who Initiated Other Non-Glucagon-Like Peptide 1 Receptor Agonists Based Glucose Lowering Drugs
- **Sponsor:** AstraZeneca (Industry) — collaborator: IQVIA Pvt. Ltd (Industry)
- **Enrollment:** 24,000 (estimated)
- **Status:** Recruiting

Pan-European retrospective database study of pancreatic cancer risk with exenatide vs. other non-GLP-1 RA-based glucose-lowering drugs, across 7 European countries.

For reference, the next two largest recruiting industry-sponsored trials by enrollment are NCT06647888 (Daewoong Pharmaceutical Co. LTD., 12,000) and NCT07064473 (Boehringer Ingelheim, 11,800).
